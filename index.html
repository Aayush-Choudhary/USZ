sickrit studio · teaser concept

usz.

A 1-minute teaser for a new way of social media. Moodboard, direction, and how we'd make it.

01 · the brief

a new way of social media.

USZ is a startup. Nobody knows it yet, so the teaser can't explain features. Its job is to make people feel the shift, understand one idea, and want to see more.

  • One idea per video. No feature tour.
  • Hook in 3 seconds. It plays in a feed, often on mute.
  • Feels new, not corporate. Human, playful, confident.
02 · directions we looked at

four ways to tell it.

cinematic

Live-action feel, depth, light, slow camera. Premium, but heavy on budget and shoot time.

big · costly

dramatic

High contrast, bold pacing, strong sound. Great for impact, risky for a friendly social brand.

loud · intense

fully 2d

Flat, graphic, fast to iterate. Clean and ownable, but can feel cold without people in it.

flexible · safe

2d + video

2D graphics and UI layered over real footage and faces. Graphic clarity with human warmth.

recommended
03 · recommended approach

2d with video. 1 minute.

  • Real people, real moments carry the emotion. 2D carries the idea and the product feel.
  • Startup-friendly. Light shoot, most of the value comes from design and motion.
  • Built to be cut down. 30s and 15s cut-downs, 9:16 and stills come straight from the same assets.

One minute gives room to set up the shift, show the idea and land the reveal. Everything in the moodboard next is pulled toward this mix.

04 · rough 1 minute flow

how it plays.

0–8shook

A familiar feed moment, then something feels off.

8–22sthe shift

The old way cracks. 2D shapes break into the footage.

22–50sthe new way

People and UI fragments move together. The idea of USZ, shown not explained.

50–60sreveal

Logo, line, date or waitlist.

A starting structure, open to change once we lock the script.
